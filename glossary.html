glossary

knitting terms

CO = cast on

BO = bind off

k = knit

p = purl

yo = yarn over

ssk = slip, slip, knit

k2tog = knit 2 together

p2tog = purl 2 together

m1L = make 1 left; i left it out front

m1R = make 1 right; i'll be right back

m1Lp = make 1 left purlwise

m1Rp = make 1 right purlwise

psso = pass slipped stitch over

kfb = knit front and back

wyif = with yarn in front

wyib = with yarn in back

pm = place marker

sm = slip marker

rm = remove marker



my basic sock pattern

using US1 dpns/addi flexiflips and sock weight yarn

CO 64 sts, join in the round

k2 p2 ribbing for 12 rounds

k in stockinette until desired length

heel flap

place 32 sts on needle 1, and work back and forth on needle 1

row 1: s1, k1 across (s1 as if to purl wyib)

row 2: s1, p across all remaining sts

repeat rows 1 and 2 for 32 rows total, and then work row 1 once more for a total of 33 rows

turning the heel

row 1: p to 2 sts beyond center (18 sts), p2tog, p1, turn

row 2: s1, k5, k2tog, k1 turn

row 3: s1, p to 1 st before last turn, p2tog, p1, turn

row 4: s1, k to 1 st before last turn, k2tog, k1, turn

repeat rows 3 and 4 until all sts at the ends are uesd up (18 sts remaining)

gusset

on the same needle, pick up and knit 16 sts along the side of the heel flap

k across the next 32 sts

puck up and knit 16 sts along the other side of the heel flap

k 9 sts to the center of the heel, place BOR marker

needle 1: 25 sts

needle 2: 32 sts

needle 3: 25 sts

row 1: k across

row 2:

needle 1: k to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1

needle 2: k across

needle 3: k1, ssk, k to end of round

repeat rows 1 and 2 for 18 rows total (64 sts remaining)

k 46 rounds of stockinette

toe shaping

row 1:

needle 1: k to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1

needle 2: k1, ssk, k to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1

needle 3: k1, ssk, k across

row 2: k across

repeat rows 1 and 2 for 20 rows total (24 sts remaining)

bind off with kitchener stitch
