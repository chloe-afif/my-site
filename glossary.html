glossary

knitting terms

CO = cast on

BO = bind off

k = knit

p = purl

yo = yarn over

ssk = slip, slip, knit

k2tog = knit 2 together

p2tog = purl 2 together

m1L = make 1 left; i left it out front

m1R = make 1 right; i'll be right back

m1Lp = make 1 left purlwise

m1Rp = make 1 right purlwise

psso = pass slipped stitch over

kfb = knit front and back

wyif = with yarn in front

wyib = with yarn in back

pm = place marker

sm = slip marker

rm = remove marker



my basic sock pattern

using US1 dpns/addi flexiflips and sock weight yarn

CO 64 sts, join in the round

k2 p2 ribbing for 12 rounds

k in stockinette until desired length
